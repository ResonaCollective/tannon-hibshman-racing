About

Tannon Hibshman

22-year-old road racer from Glen Rock, Pennsylvania, competing in SCCA ITS class road racing and Mid-Atlantic hillclimb events.

The Story

How It Started

Tannon Hibshman grew up around cars, and racing them was the natural next step. He drives the #53 Mazda RX-7 in SCCA's Improved Touring Sport (ITS) class. His season is a mix of Mid-Atlantic Road Racing Series (MARRS) weekends at Summit Point Motorsports Park in West Virginia and the Pagoda and Duryea hillclimbs in Reading, Pennsylvania.

On Track

Smooth and Consistent

Tannon focuses on carrying the right speed through each corner, hitting his marks, and doing it the same way every lap. That consistency is a big part of why he was on the podium at every race in 2026. His motto, Never Give Up, is written on the car's center console where he can see it from the driver's seat.

2026: Officially Licensed

From Novice to Race Car Driver

Every SCCA road racer starts on a Novice Permit, which is basically a learner's permit for racing. New drivers have to show they're safe and consistent in wheel-to-wheel racing before they can move up. Tannon earned his full SCCA competition license during the 2026 season, so he's now officially a licensed race car driver.

Goals

Looking Ahead to 2027

The #53 RX-7 is getting new tires this winter. Next up is 2027, Tannon's first full season as a licensed driver, and he plans to be back on track racing all year.
