About

Tannon Hibshman

22-year-old road racer from Glen Rock, Pennsylvania, competing in SCCA ITS class road racing and Mid-Atlantic hillclimb events.

The Story

Built On Focus, Not Flash

Tannon Hibshman grew up around cars and competition, and that early exposure grew into a genuine passion for wheel-to-wheel racing. Today he competes behind the wheel of the #53 Mazda RX-7 in SCCA's Improved Touring Sport (ITS) class, running a full season of Mid-Atlantic Road Racing Series (MARRS) events at Summit Point Motorsports Park alongside the historic Pagoda and Duryea hillclimbs in his home region of Reading, Pennsylvania.
